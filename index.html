Scope

Requisition list

Required = the loadout allocation for every group present, by its doctrine position, plus the full allocation of any position the Task Force quantity says should exist but has no group yet. Group shortfall adds up each group's own gap (a surplus in one group doesn't cancel another group's gap). Part of that can be covered by moving stock the TF already owns — the reserve, unassigned stock and (unless unticked) other groups' surplus and stock in groups with no position. The rest is the net to requisition: new stock the TF doesn't have anywhere. Click a line for the per-group breakdown.

Ship moves & swaps

Compares every ship's group tag with where it physically is, for the groups in scope. A stand-in is a reserve ship lent to a group: it carries the group tag and the reserve tag, and counts for the group. Fill gap — undamaged ships (regulars + stand-ins) are below the allocation: tag reserve ships as stand-ins, preferably ones already aboard (Tag as stand-in). Move aboard — tagged to the group, undamaged, not inside its flagship. Release stand-in — the regulars aboard cover the allocation again (a repaired ship is back), so remove the group tag and return it to the reserve; stand-ins not yet moved aboard are released first. Damaged uses the damage rule chosen in Scope. Stray — inside the flagship but tagged to another group. Reserve aboard — undamaged reserve / untagged ships carried by the flagship beyond any gap (one line per type). Only ships are listed; flagships count toward their own line.
